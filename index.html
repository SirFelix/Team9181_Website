Ship's Manifest

Who we are

Mission

Build confidence, not just robots

We're an all-girls FRC team because representation in STEM shouldn't be an afterthought. Every member gets real hands-on time on mechanical, electrical, programming, and strategy — no roles reserved by default.

Program

FIRST Robotics Competition

Each January we get a new game and six weeks to design, build, wire, and program a robot from scratch, then compete at regional events alongside hundreds of teams worldwide.
